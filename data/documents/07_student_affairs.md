# CUI Abbottabad — Student Affairs
Source: https://www.cuiatd.edu.pk/about/offices-and-administration/student-affairs/

## Student Affairs role
Student Affairs operates as a one-window service for academic and administrative student needs. Applications can be submitted through SAPS/student portal and are forwarded to relevant offices.

## Services and published fees
- Character Certificate
- English Proficiency Certificate
- Studying Certificate
- Fee Equivalence Certificate for WWB students
- Course Studied Certificate
- Provisional Certificate
- Migration Certificate for graduate students
- Document Verification
- Transcript and Degree issuance
- Fee/security refund applications
- Lost student ID card re-issuance
- Admission cancellation clearance
- Re-admission cases for DI students

## Published processing fees on the page
- Many certificates: Rs. 200 normal / Rs. 400 urgent
- Migration Certificate: Rs. 2,500
- Document verification: Rs. 2,000 per document
- Transcript and degree issuance: Rs. 10,000
- Lost ID card: Rs. 500 plus affidavit

These amounts are source-specific and may change. Always cite the current Student Affairs page when answering.

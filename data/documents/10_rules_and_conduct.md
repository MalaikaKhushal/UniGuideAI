# CUI Abbottabad — Student Discipline and Conduct
Source: https://www.cuiatd.edu.pk/students/code-of-conduct/

## Discipline regulations
The official page publishes the CUI Students Discipline Regulations, 2006 and states that they apply to all students of the Institute.

The page identifies competent authorities including:
- Rector
- Discipline Committee
- Directors
- Campus Discipline Committee
- Provost
- Officer In-charge of Student Affairs

The page also contains conduct and hostel-related rules. Examples include keeping rooms clean, reporting staff misbehavior to the warden, restrictions on unauthorized overnight guests, and restrictions on electric heaters and air conditioners in hostel rooms.

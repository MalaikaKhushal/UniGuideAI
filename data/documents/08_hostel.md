# CUI Abbottabad — Hostel
Source: https://www.cuiatd.edu.pk/students/hostel/

## Facilities
The official hostel page states:
- Two blocks (Salman Firdaus Hostel and an old block) accommodate approximately 666 male students.
- One block accommodates more than 120 female students.
- Rooms can accommodate three occupants.
- Rooms have three study tables, three beds, and one large closet.
- Boys hostel: badminton court, two mess options, paid weekly laundry, paid 24/7 Wi-Fi, gym.
- Girls hostel: three meals a day in hostel mess, laundry, paid Wi-Fi, lawns, 24-hour lady warden, independent gate, and ladies gym.
- Students need permission to go outside the institute premises; the page specifically states female students require approval of the warden and Provost to go outside the main gate.

## Caution
Hostel rules, capacity, fees, and facilities are time-sensitive.

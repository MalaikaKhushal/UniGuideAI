## Other information
- Additional semester: registration fee plus per-credit-hour fee.
- The page states Rs. 6,000 per credit hour for undergraduate and Master level programs and Rs. 4,000 for MS/PhD.
- Degree fee listed: Rs. 10,000 on completion of degree.
- The page states fee rates are subject to revision in subsequent semesters.

## Hostel fee
The Fall 2026 fee page lists hostel fee without mess as Rs. 53,000 plus Rs. 2,000 security, shown as a total of Rs. 55,000.

IMPORTANT: Fees are time-sensitive. Always attach the source URL and date/session to fee answers.

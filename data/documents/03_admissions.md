## General procedure
The official admission procedure describes these stages:
1. Online admission application
2. Entrance test
3. Merit list
4. Interview where applicable
5. Provisional admission offer and fee payment
6. Provisional admission formalities
7. Acceptance of provisional offer
8. Orientation

Candidates must apply within the advertised deadline. The official procedure states that late admission forms are not entertained.

## Merit formula
Bachelor:
- Matric/equivalence: 10%
- Intermediate/equivalence: 40%
- NTS NAT-I: 50%

Master:
- Matric/equivalence: 10%
- Intermediate/equivalence: 15%
- Bachelor/equivalence: 30%
- NTS NAT-II: 45%

## Online application
The official How to Apply page describes:
- Sign up on the online admissions system.
- Authenticate email.
- Log in.
- Complete application sections.
- Upload required documents.
- Pay the challan.
- NTS test is required for application consideration according to the page.
- Submit the application.

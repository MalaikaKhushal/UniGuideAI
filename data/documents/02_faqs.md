# CUI Abbottabad — Frequently Asked Questions
Source: https://cuiatd.edu.pk/faqs/

## Admissions
- Undergraduate and graduate admissions are held in Spring and Fall sessions according to the official FAQ.
- Applicants can create an online admission account using a valid email address.
- The FAQ states that applicants may apply based on SSC/Matric marks when the HSSC result is awaited.
- The FAQ gives the following admission-test merit weightage:
  - Bachelor: 10% Matric/equivalence + 40% Intermediate/equivalence + 50% NTS NAT-I.
  - Master: 10% Matric/equivalence + 15% Intermediate/equivalence + 30% Bachelor/equivalence + 45% NTS NAT-II.
- For questions about the application process, the FAQ lists admissions@cuiatd.edu.pk and telephone contacts 0092-992-383596 and 0092-3466111189.

## Scholarship information
The FAQ directs students to the official Scholarship and Financial Aid pages for current scholarship schemes.

## Important
FAQ information can become outdated. The chatbot should retrieve the latest official admission/fee/scholarship page when a current date, deadline, or amount is requested.

# CUI Abbottabad — Transport
Source: https://www.cuiatd.edu.pk/students/transport-2/

## Service
CUI Abbottabad provides student transport on allocated routes in Abbottabad and Mansehra and states that service has also been extended to Havelian and Haripur.

## Published route charges (page says Spring 2023 onward)
- Haripur: Rs. 22,000 per semester for each side (pick or drop)
- Havelian: Rs. 12,500 per semester for each side
- Local route: Rs. 50 per person per side, paid on the spot, within Abbottabad City
- Mansehra route: Rs. 16,500 per semester for each side

The page lists local destinations including Nawan Shehr, Fowara Chowk, PMA Link Road, Jinnahabad, and Main Mansehra Road.

## Office
The transport office is near the administration block parking area. The page lists office timing as 9:00 am to 5:00 pm except Saturday and Sunday and provides extension 392 for further information.

IMPORTANT: These are dated published charges and must not be treated as current without checking the official page.

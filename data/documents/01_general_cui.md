# CUI Abbottabad — General Information
Source: https://cuiatd.edu.pk/about/introduction/
Source: https://cuiatd.edu.pk/

## Identity
COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI), Abbottabad Campus is located in Abbottabad, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.

The campus website states that CUI attained degree-awarding status through Presidential Ordinance No. XXXVIII on 12 August 2000.

The CUI Abbottabad homepage states that the campus offers undergraduate and graduate programs and provides facilities including a library, research resources, scholarships, and student services.

## Permanent Dhamtour Campus
The official campus introduction describes the permanent campus at Dhamtour and states that the master plan includes academic blocks, mosque, girls' and boys' hostel facilities, library, auditorium, sports gymnasium, and student service center.

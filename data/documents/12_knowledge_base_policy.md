# UniGuide AI — Knowledge Base Rules

## Primary source policy
Use official CUI/COMSATS Abbottabad sources as the primary authority for university facts.

## Source priority
1. Current official CUI Abbottabad page/document
2. Official CUI document/form
3. Older official CUI material only when clearly labelled as historical

## Time-sensitive information
Fees, admission dates, scholarship amounts, transport charges, hostel fees, contacts, and policies can change. Store source URL and source date/session with every document.

## Hallucination protection
If the retrieved university sources do not contain enough evidence, the assistant should say that the information was not found in the verified university knowledge base instead of inventing an answer.

## Answer format
Where possible:
- Give a concise answer.
- Mention the relevant CUI office/process.
- Provide source title/URL.
- For time-sensitive facts, mention the applicable session/date.

## Example
Question: "BSSE ki Fall 2026 fee kitni hai?"
Answer should use the Fall 2026 fee source and distinguish admission/registration/tuition/total rather than giving an unsupported number.

# CUI Abbottabad — Scholarships and Financial Assistance
Source: https://www.cuiatd.edu.pk/admissions/scholarships/

## Examples of published scholarship schemes
The official page lists multiple internal scholarship and concession schemes, including:
- COMSATS Abbottabad Merit Scholarships (CAMS)
- Merit-based categories for eligible programs
- Alumni scholarships for MS/MBA/PhD
- Haroon Rashid Scholarship for students attaining 4.00 GPA/CGPA
- CUI Need Based Scholarship
- Sibling Concession and other categories described on the official page

## Need-based scholarship
The official page states that financial assistance is intended for extremely needy students whose financial status changes during studies or who face circumstances that hinder continuation of studies. It describes assessment through the Student Development Center and Campus Scholarship Evaluation Committee.

## Important restrictions
The official scholarship page includes conditions concerning simultaneous scholarships, normal program duration, refunds, continuation, and scholarship eligibility. Because these conditions can change, the chatbot should cite the exact current scholarship page rather than relying on memory.

## Sibling concession
An official sibling application form states that undergraduate students can be eligible for a Rs. 16,000 fee concession from Spring 2024 subject to the listed conditions. This is a dated document and should not be presented as a current amount without checking the latest official policy/form.
